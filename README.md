# Logistics Data Platform

## About the Project

This project aims to build a data platform for a fictional B2B logistics company that works with multiple carriers.

The platform will centralize, standardize, and process shipment and tracking data, providing reliable information for monitoring logistics operations.

## Business Problem

The company receives data from multiple carriers, each using different formats, structures, and standards.

This fragmentation creates data quality and standardization issues, including inconsistent status naming, duplicate records, missing data, and inconsistencies in tracking events.

As a result, the company struggles to monitor the complete shipment lifecycle and reliably measure delays, SLA compliance, and carrier performance.

## Stakeholders

### Logistics Operations
- Monitor shipments in transit, delivered, and delayed.
- Identify shipments without recent tracking updates.
- Monitor SLA compliance.
- Identify recurring issues across routes and carriers.

### Carrier Management
- Compare carrier performance.
- Monitor SLA compliance.
- Analyze delays and transit times.
- Evaluate performance by route.

### Management and Leadership
- Monitor key operational indicators.
- Identify logistics bottlenecks.
- Compare carrier performance.
- Monitor operational performance over time.

### Data Team
- Centralize data from multiple carriers.
- Standardize schemas and shipment statuses.
- Ensure data quality and historical consistency.
- Provide reliable datasets for analysis.

## Business Questions

- How many shipments are currently in transit, delivered, or delayed?
- What percentage of deliveries are completed on time?
- What is the average transit time?
- How do carriers compare in terms of SLA compliance?
- Which routes have the highest incidence of delays?
- Which shipments have not received recent tracking updates?
- What data quality issues exist within the tracking events?

## KPIs

- Total Shipments
- Delivered Shipments
- Delayed Shipments
- On-Time Delivery Rate (OTD)
- Average Transit Time
- Average Delay
- OTD by Carrier
- OTD by Lane
- Shipments Without Recent Tracking
- Tracking Data Completeness Rate

## Scope

The project includes:

- Data from multiple carriers.
- Shipment data.
- Transportation routes.
- Tracking events.
- Data standardization.
- Data quality validation.
- Shipment history.
- SLA analysis.
- Delay analysis.
- Carrier performance analysis.

## Out of Scope

The first version of the project will not cover:

- Inventory management.
- Internal warehouse operations.
- Inventory control.
- Product/SKU management.
- Route optimization.
- Financial management and billing.
- Freight cost analysis.

These areas may be considered in future versions of the project.
